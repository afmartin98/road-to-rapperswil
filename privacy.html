Privacy Policy

Triathlon training app by Martin Affolter, Zürich, Switzerland. Effective 24 October 2026.

In short: the app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your training and health data stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see any of it.

1. What the app does

The app turns your training plan into a calendar, matches it with the workouts you record, and shows your fitness, recovery and race preparation. All calculations happen on your device.

2. Data the developer collects

None. The app does not send any data to the developer or to any server operated by the developer. There is no user account, no sign-up and no usage statistics.

3. Where your data is stored

DataWhere it lives
Training plans, recorded workouts, your notes and edits, profile and settingsOn your device, and in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac stay in sync. Only you can access it.
Plan files (.xlsx) and Zwift workout files (.zwo)In the app's folder in your iCloud Drive (or on your device if iCloud Drive is off).
Per-second workout recordingsOn each device only.
Keys you enter (Garmin, Strava, intervals.icu)In your iCloud Keychain, encrypted by Apple.
Data read from Apple HealthUsed on your device while the app runs. It is not stored in iCloud and not sent anywhere.

4. Services you choose to connect

Connections are optional and use your own keys or logins. The app talks to these services directly from your device; the developer is not involved and receives nothing. Each service's own privacy policy applies to the data it holds.

  • Garmin Connect (read only, with your personal API key): activities and their recordings, sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, Body Battery, stress, heart rate zones and fitness age.
  • Strava (read only, through your own Strava API application and login): activities and their recordings.
  • intervals.icu (with your API key): the app adds your planned bike workouts to your intervals.icu calendar so they reach Zwift. It removes them again when you take them off Zwift.
  • Apple Health (read only, with your permission): resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and weight. The app never writes to Apple Health.

5. Notifications

Reminders and workout results are local notifications created on your device. No push service run by the developer is involved.

6. Beta testing with TestFlight

If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share with the developer the crash reports and the feedback (including screenshots) that you choose to send, plus your name and email address as given in TestFlight. See Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete the app from your devices to remove local data.
  • Remove its iCloud data in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.
  • Revoke the keys you created in Garmin Connect, Strava and intervals.icu at any time in those services.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
